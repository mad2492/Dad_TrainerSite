# Dad Discovery Questions

Purpose: guide the first conversation without making it feel like a software interview.

## Easy Opening Questions

- What would you want people to know about you before they ever call you?
- What kinds of clients are your best fit?
- What do clients usually need the most help staying consistent with?
- What do you wish new clients understood earlier?

## Website Questions

- If someone found you online, what should they see first?
- What services should be listed clearly?
- Do you want to show pricing, or should people contact you first?
- Do you have testimonials or client results we can use?
- Do you want photos of you, the gym, client transformations, or none of that yet?

## Client Portal Questions

- Would clients actually log in to see diet plans and notes?
- What would be worth putting in a client profile?
- What should clients never be able to change themselves?
- Would progress photos be useful, annoying, or too sensitive?
- Would a simple text-style update be better than a formal portal at first?

## Trainer Workflow Questions

- What would you want to update from your phone after a session?
- What information do you need to find quickly?
- What would be annoying to type on an iPhone?
- Would voice notes or short preset buttons help later?

## Payment Questions

- What payment methods work best right now?
- Do you want the website to mention payment methods?
- Would payment links help or just add fees?
- Is payment tracking useful even if payment processing stays outside the site?
