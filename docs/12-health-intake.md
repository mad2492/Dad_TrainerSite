# Health Intake

Purpose: define the basic medical/readiness information Fonseca Fitness should collect before creating workout or nutrition plans.

## Why This Matters

Pete noted that they need to know about medical conditions such as blood pressure, cardiac issues, arthritis, or any condition that could affect training.

This is important for:

- Client safety
- Personalizing workouts and nutrition
- Knowing when to modify exercises
- Knowing when a client should talk to a medical professional before starting
- Avoiding one-size-fits-all programming

## Recommended MVP Intake

Before a client starts, collect:

- Blood pressure concerns
- Cardiac/heart issues
- Arthritis
- Injuries or chronic pain
- Diabetes or blood sugar concerns, if disclosed
- Surgeries or movement restrictions
- Medications that affect exercise, heart rate, blood pressure, appetite, or hydration, if disclosed
- Doctor restrictions or clearance status
- Emergency contact
- Current activity level
- Primary goal

## Screening Pattern

Use a simple readiness questionnaire modeled after common fitness-industry screening patterns.

Reference patterns:

- PAR-Q+ is a common pre-participation screening tool used to identify whether a person should answer follow-up questions or consult a medical professional before physical activity.
- ACSM preparticipation screening focuses on identifying cardiovascular, metabolic, renal, and symptom-related risks before exercise participation.

## Important Boundary

Fonseca Fitness should not diagnose conditions or provide medical advice.

The intake should help Pete and Lisa:

- Understand limitations
- Modify exercise selection
- Avoid known problem movements
- Request medical clearance when needed
- Recommend the client consult a physician when a condition or symptom is outside training scope

## Product Implications

The portal should eventually support:

- Health intake form
- PAR-Q-style readiness questions
- Medical condition notes visible to Pete/Lisa
- Red flags/clearance-needed status
- Client acknowledgement that they are responsible for accurate health information
- Private storage and restricted access

## Privacy Notes

Health information is sensitive.

Do not collect real client medical details until the app has:

- Secure authentication
- Access control
- Private storage
- Clear retention/deletion rules
- A plan for who can see the information

For the static mockup, use fake sample data only.
