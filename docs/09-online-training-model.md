# Online Training Model

Purpose: define the online training offer before building software around it.

Dad's key goal is not just to organize current in-person clients. He wants a way to serve more clients online without adding more in-person hours to the day.

## Core Opportunity

In-person training is limited by time. One client can consume a large block of the day. Online training could support more clients from home if the workflow is simple, repeatable, and still personal.

Target idea to explore:

- 20 to 30 online clients
- Managed from home
- Individualized plans
- Communication when needed
- Less time per client than in-person training
- No cookie-cutter program

## What Online Training Could Include

### Diet-First Coaching

- Custom diet plan
- Periodic diet updates
- Reminders
- Check-ins
- Client questions

### Workout Programming

- Workout plan
- Exercise notes
- Session alternatives
- Progression guidance
- Optional video links later

### Hybrid Coaching

- In-person training for local clients
- Online support between sessions
- Online-only coaching for people who cannot train in person

## Questions To Answer Next

- Should online clients get workouts, diets, or both?
- Should there be different levels of online coaching?

- Regular reminders

- Message access with boundaries

### Full Online Coaching

- Diet plan
- Workout plan

### Hybrid Client Support

- In-person training

- Reminders

## Product Implications

The platform should prioritize:

- Diet plan creation and updates
- Reminder management
- Client check-ins
- Communication requests
- Dashboard for who needs attention
- Reusable structure without generic, cookie-cutter plans

Avoid early:

- Complicated payments
- Overbuilt exercise libraries
- Too many notification types
- Unlimited messaging expectations
- Features that require lots of typing from an iPhone
