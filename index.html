The promise

Built Around the Person, Not a Template

Fonseca Fitness is for people who want a real plan, clear direction, and accountability without feeling like they were handed the same program as everyone else.

Every client starts with their goal, experience level, health background, schedule, and comfort level. From there, Pete and Lisa recommend the right coaching path.

Simple idea: give online clients the structure they need each week while keeping the personal coaching style Pete and Lisa are known for.

How it works

A Simple Weekly Coaching Rhythm

The online program should feel clear for clients and manageable for Pete and Lisa.

1

Start With a Consultation

The client shares their goal, training history, schedule, and what they need help with most.

2

Complete Health Intake

Before a plan is written, Pete and Lisa review injuries, blood pressure, cardiac history, arthritis, and other important conditions.

3

Receive the Weekly Plan

The client gets workouts, nutrition guidance, exercise demo links, and notes that explain what to focus on.

4

Check In Once a Week

The client sends weight, new injuries or concerns, and optional progress photos. Pete and Lisa adjust the plan when needed.

Review pitch

The Idea Behind the Website

This tab keeps the planning notes visible while the website draft evolves. The goal is to support how Pete and Lisa already coach, not force them into complicated software.

What we learned

The business has trust but needs reach

New clients usually come through word of mouth. The online side can help more people find Fonseca Fitness and make online coaching easier to manage from home.

Main pain point

Diets are repeated and rewritten

Diet plans and reminders are the things Pete most often sends and resends. A simple client profile could make that easier without changing his coaching style.

Big opportunity

20 to 30 online clients

In-person training is limited by hours in the day. Online coaching could create more income without needing every client to take up a full in-person time block.

Non-negotiable

Keep it simple

The platform has to work from an iPhone, avoid clutter, and make weekly online coaching manageable instead of becoming another thing to babysit.
