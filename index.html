A practical proposal

A Simple Online Platform for Your Training Business

You already know how to train people. This idea is about making the business side feel more organized, more professional, and easier to handle from your phone.

Start small: first, a clean website people can find and trust.
Phone first: built so you can use it comfortably from your iPhone.
No pressure on payments: Zelle, Venmo, Cash App, or cash can stay exactly as-is.

The idea

What This Is, and What It Is Not

This is not about replacing your experience, your coaching style, or the way you work with people. You have been doing this a long time, and that is the whole point: the website should support what already makes you good.

The simple version is this: a professional website for new people who want to learn about you, plus a private client area where current clients can see their diet plans, notes, and progress.

Instead of everything living in texts, scratch pads, and memory, the important pieces would have one clean place to live.

For clients

What Clients Would See

Clients would not need anything fancy. They would open a link on their phone and see the parts of your coaching that matter to them.

Website

A clear first impression

Who you are, what you offer, what kind of trainer you are, and how someone can contact you.

Plans

Their diet plan

A simple place to check what they should be following without asking you to resend it.

Sessions

Workout recaps

Quick notes after sessions: what they did, what improved, and what to focus on next.

Progress

Check-ins and results

Weight, measurements, photos, goals, and progress notes if that becomes useful later.

Trust

Testimonials

Real client stories and results so new people understand the value of your experience.

For you

What You Could Do From Your iPhone

The trainer side should be simple: big enough to tap, easy to read, and focused on the things you actually use.

  • Open your client list quickly.
  • Tap a client and see their diet plan, notes, and recent updates.
  • Update a plan after a check-in or conversation.
  • Add a quick workout recap after a session.
  • Leave private notes for yourself.
  • See who needs a follow-up.
  • Keep the important details in one place instead of scattered across texts and paper.

Payments

Payments Can Stay the Way They Are

If Zelle, Venmo, Cash App, checks, or cash are working for you, there is no need to change that at the beginning.

The website and client portal do not need online card payments to be useful. If payments ever become part of it, we would look at the fees first and keep it simple.

Questions

What I Would Want Your Opinion On

Before building anything real, I would want your honest take on questions like these:

  1. How do new clients usually find you right now?
  2. What do you currently send clients most often: diets, workouts, notes, reminders, or something else?
  3. What gets annoying to resend or rewrite?
  4. Would it help to have one place for each client's plan and notes?
  5. Would your clients actually log in to see their information, or would they rather keep texting?
  6. What would make this useful for you instead of just another thing to manage?
  7. What should absolutely stay the same about how you run your business?
  8. What would you want people to understand about you when they first find you online?

First step

A Clickable Demo, Not a Full Build

The first step would not be a big project. I would make a simple clickable demo that you can open on your phone.

You could tap around, see what the website and client area might feel like, and tell me what is useful, what is annoying, and what is missing.

If it fits how you work, we keep going. If it does not, we change it or drop it. The point is to help your business, not force you into software you do not want.
