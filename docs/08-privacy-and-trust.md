# Privacy and Trust

Purpose: make sure client information is treated carefully before any portal is built.

## Data Sensitivity

Potentially sensitive information:

- Diet plans
- Weight
- Measurements
- Progress photos
- Health limitations or injuries
- Payment status
- Private trainer notes

## Early Rule

Do not put real private client data into the project until authentication, access control, and storage decisions are clear.

## Public Website Safety

Public website content is safer when it includes:

- General services
- Business story
- Contact information
- Testimonials with permission
- Photos with permission

Avoid publishing:

- Client names without permission
- Transformation photos without permission
- Specific health details
- Anything that makes a client identifiable without consent

## Portal Safety Questions

- Who can see each client's information?
- What can Dad see?
- What can clients edit?
- What should be private to Dad only?
- How are photos stored?
- How would data be deleted if a client asks?
- What happens if Dad loses or replaces his phone?

## Trust Principles

- Keep private information private.
- Use real permissions for testimonials and photos.
- Keep the portal simple enough that mistakes are unlikely.
- Avoid storing anything we do not need.
